Privacy Policy — Tend

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Tend (“the app”) is developed by Hriday Pratim Bharali. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: Tend collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. Tend does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no account or sign-in, and it requests no internet permission, so it is technically unable to send any data off your device.

Data stored on your device

The things you choose to tend, your tending history, your streak, and your theme preference are stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage. This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): used only to show a gentle local reminder of today’s one thing to tend. These notifications are generated on your device. You can disable them at any time in your system settings. The app works fully without this permission.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data, because we do not collect any.

Third-party services

Tend contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Tend does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at the contact email listed on the app’s Google Play store page.
